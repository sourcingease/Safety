Tax Rules
Annual slabs: monthly gross × 12 is taxed as fixed amount + rate % of income above the slab's "from", then divided by 12. Percent: rate % of monthly gross (optional wage cap / amount cap). Fixed: a flat monthly amount. Employer rules are company costs and are not deducted from pay.
