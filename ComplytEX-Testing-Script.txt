======================================================================
SECTION 1 – Login & Authentication
======================================================================

TC-LOGIN-01 – Valid login (Owner)
Objective: Verify that a valid Owner account can sign in.
Preconditions: Owner demo user exists (owner@demo.example / DemoPass123!).
Steps:
 1. Navigate to /login.html.
 2. Enter Owner email and password.
 3. Click "Sign In".
Expected Result:
 - Green status "Signed in!".
 - Redirect to /dashboard.
 - Session cookie is created.

TC-LOGIN-02 – Invalid password
Objective: Validate error handling for wrong password.
Steps:
 1. Open /login.html.
 2. Enter valid email with incorrect password.
 3. Click "Sign In".
Expected Result:
 - Red error message (e.g. "Login failed").
 - User remains on login page.

TC-LOGIN-03 – Demo account quick login
Objective: Confirm demo account buttons work.
Steps:
 1. On /login.html locate Demo Accounts.
 2. Click "Login as HR Manager".
Expected Result:
 - Email/password auto-fill.
 - User is logged in and redirected to dashboard.
